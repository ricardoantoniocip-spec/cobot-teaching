Tell us your task.

Describe one task you would like a cobot to learn. We reply with whether it is a good fit, what we would need from you and what it would take.

What helps us most

  • What the robot should pick, and where it should put it
  • How many different parts or variants there are
  • How often the task changes
  • Which robot and gripper you have, if any
  • A phone video of someone doing the task, if you can share one

This form does not store anything. It writes your message so you can send it to us.

For example: pick 6 different brackets from bins and place them in a kit tray with 12 slots.
