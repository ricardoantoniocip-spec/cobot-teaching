## Suggested order
1. Now: email HighTechXL (entrepreneur@hightechxl.com) and ask whether the Fall 2026 cohort still takes applicants and whether there is a part-time path. Ask about the next cohort too.
2. Before 20 Dec 2026: decide whether to register an eenmanszaak so WBSO 2027 can start on 1 Jan (it pays for your own R&D hours on the arm PoC).
3. Spring 2027: MIT Zuid feasibility (€20k) for the "new task in a week" pilot study. Be ready on opening day.
4. Once a PT pilot customer exists: a PT2030 R&D project with that customer, plus the next Portugal Ventures call.
