# Grants, accelerators and funding (checked 2026-11-01)

Monthly check. Fit = a solo, part-time founder with no company yet (or a new NL eenmanszaak / PT company) building cobot teaching software. Verify on the official page before applying.

## Deadlines in the next 60 days (to 31 Dec 2026)

| Deadline | Program | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 31 Dec 2026 (self-employed, no staff); 20 Dec 2026 (employers) | WBSO 2027, NL | Apply for a period starting 1 Jan 2027. Needs a registered eenmanszaak first (KvK) and a plan for at least 500 R&D hours in 2027. Check ASML side-activity rules before registering. [Hezelburcht, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.hezelburcht.com/nieuws/wbso-2027-aanvraagperiode-start-nu/) |

Just outside the window: PT2030 STEP Inovação Produtiva Digital closes 4 Jan 2027 (low fit, see table).

## Changes since 2026-10-27

- **NEW finding, StartUP Voucher (PT):** last month's "up to €200k" was wrong. It is €900/month for up to 9 months, for graduates up to 29 who are not working, from Norte/Centro/Alentejo. Not eligible while employed. Last edition closed 30 Jan 2026. [ECO, 23 Jan 2026](https://eco.sapo.pt/2026/01/23/candidaturas-ao-startup-voucher-terminam-a-30-de-janeiro/)
- **HighTechXL:** the Fall 2026 deadline ("October 2026") has passed. Next cohort not announced. [HighTechXL](https://www.hightechxl.com/?p=483)
- **WBSO 2027 dates confirmed** (still pending parliament): 20 Dec employers, 31 Dec self-employed; starter rate stays 50%.
- **NEW, ANI Deep2Start vouchers (PT):** €10k + €60k non-repayable for PT startups that pass EIC Accelerator stage 1. Worth knowing for the EIC route later; current call status unknown (article from March 2025). [ANI](https://ani.pt/en/ani-launches-first-funding-call-for-deep-tech-startups/)
- **NEW, Tech Foundry Portugal:** Startup Portugal + Hello Tomorrow 4-month programme for TRL 4-5 teams; the 2026 cohort runs Sep-Dec 2026. Candidate for the 2027 edition once the SO-101 demo works. No funding amount stated. [Portugal Global, 23 Jun 2026](https://www.portugalglobal.pt/pt/noticias/2026/junho/tech-foundry-portugal-candidaturas-abertas/)
- **Portugal Ventures Call for Deep Tech:** still closed; needs a PT company in Norte/Centro with an MVP and customers. [Portugal Ventures](https://www.portugalventures.pt/en/calls/call-for-deep-tech/)
