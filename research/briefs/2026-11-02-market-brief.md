# Market brief 2026-11-02 (26 Oct - 1 Nov 2026)

## What matters for you this week
- **A direct European rival came out of stealth.** Machenta (London) raised a $40M seed to let robots learn a new task after seeing it once, trained only in simulation. Same promise as yours, much more money. Your edge has to be the deployment service and existing cobots (UR, FANUC CRX, Doosan), not the model.
- **Money keeps flooding into robot learning.** Physical Intelligence is reportedly raising at least $1B at about $20B. Good for your fundraising story later; it also means "learn from demonstration" models get cheaper and better for you to build on.
- **Nothing new in Portugal or the Netherlands** this week, and no new grant calls (see funding/grants-2026-11-01.md).

## Implications
1. Your pitch should stress what these labs don't do: fixed-price deployment on cobots the customer already owns, done in a week, with a verified native program (URScript).
2. Add Machenta and Bleu to the competitor list in docs/context.md.
3. A good interview question now: "Would you rather buy a learning model, or pay per task that works?"
