# Market brief 2026-11-09 (2 - 8 Nov 2026)

## What matters for you this week
- **A US company now sells robot work by the hour or by the pick, trained from human demonstrations.** Tutor Intelligence raised $75M (Series B, led by a16z). No upfront purchase, no long-term contract. This is proof that pay-per-result pricing works, but it competes with a fixed price per task. Their market is US warehouses with their own robots, not existing cobots in EU factories.
- **The Physical AI Growth Lab (UnternehmerTUM, Munich) is equity-free and closes 13 Nov.** It needs a working MVP and a technical co-founder, so skip it this round. Aim for the next cohort once the SO-101 demo runs.
- **Quiet week for Portugal and the Netherlands.** I found no new cobot or integrator news in either country. SPRIND's robotics challenge (up to €7.2M per team) closed on 22 Oct.

## Implications
1. Prepare an answer to "why not pay per pick like Tutor?" One option: offer both, a fixed price for the setup and an optional per-month support fee.
2. Ask in interviews: "Would you rather pay per task set up, or per hour of robot work?"
3. Put the next Physical AI Growth Lab round in your calendar. You need a working demo and ideally a co-founder first.
