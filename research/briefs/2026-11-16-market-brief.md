# Market brief 2026-11-16 (9 - 15 Nov 2026)

## What matters for you this week
- **Path Robotics raised $300M (Series E, $2.45B valuation) for AI welding robots that adapt in real time.** It is the biggest "physical AI for manufacturing" round this week. It shows investors pay for AI that removes robot programming in factories. Path builds its own welding cells and sells in the US, so it isn't a direct rival for you.
- **INESC TEC in Porto has a project (MOBOT 2.0) where operators teach a mobile robot new tasks through AR glasses.** It is about €1.5M over 3 years with Europneumaq, and the page shows no date. That is close to your idea and right next to Germano Veiga, so ask him about it when you talk.
- **Quiet week otherwise.** I found no new rounds in Europe in your space, no news from Sereact, Wandelbots, Mowito, Acumino or Physical Intelligence, and no new grant or accelerator calls that fit you.
