# Plain video of the operator becomes robot training data (2026-11-12)

**What:** Ricardo asked for an app that takes a video of him doing a task, tracks his arm and turns it into data the SO-101 can train on. First version: MediaPipe tracks shoulder, elbow, wrist and hand in a plain video, with no markers on the body. The robot follows the pinch point between thumb and index finger, and the thumb-index gap gives the grip. The demo is cut into steps (reach, grasp, move, release), replayed in the MuJoCo twin and saved as a LeRobot episode only if the replay succeeds. Command: `python -m so101_sim from-video`. The technical choices (MediaPipe, follow the hand and not the arm angles, rebuild the motion from the steps) were proposed by Claude in the PR and are open to change.
**Why:** It needs only a phone, so he can start before the arm and the sleeve exist. Following the hand instead of copying joint angles is needed because a human arm has 7 joints and the SO-101 has 5. The step list is the same hand-over point the LLM program generator needs later (decision 2026-10-31).
**What would change it:** tracking on real videos is too poor to find the grasp (then use the ArUco hand marker or the sleeve), one camera's depth error is too large for the real arm (then add a second camera or a depth camera), or a better open tracker fits the 4 GB GPU.
