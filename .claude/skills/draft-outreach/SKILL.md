# Draft outreach (never send)

1. Pick the recipients: names Ricardo gives, or the next `to contact` rows in `customers/pipeline*.csv`. Read `docs/context.md` for who they are.
2. For each recipient, look up one specific fact about them (their website or a news item) and keep the URL.
3. Write the message:
   - Language: Portuguese for Portugal, Dutch or English for NL, English for South Africa. For Portuguese, use correct European Portuguese ("você"/formal, not Brazilian).
   - Under 80 words. One specific line about them. Ricardo is a production engineer researching how long it takes to set up a new cobot task. Ask for a 20-minute call. No product pitch, no prices.
   - Subject line for email; none for LinkedIn (and under 300 characters for a connection note).
4. Write a follow-up for 7 days later, under 40 words.
5. Save all drafts to `customers/outreach/YYYY-MM-DD.md` with recipient, channel, the fact and its URL.
6. Update `next_step` in the pipeline row to "draft ready YYYY-MM-DD". Do not change status to contacted; Ricardo does that after he sends.
7. Reply with the drafts so Ricardo can copy them from his phone.

Never send, post, schedule or email anything. Never invent a shared contact or past meeting.
