# Grant check

1. Read `docs/context.md` (situation, company status, hours) and the newest `funding/grants-*.md`.
2. For each programme in that file, plus any Ricardo names, open the official call page. Record: open or closed, deadline, amount, co-funding rate, eligibility. Link the page. Never use a search snippet as a source.
3. Go/no-go for each, against his situation today:
   - Does a solo, part-time founder qualify?
   - Does it need a company (NL eenmanszaak or BV, PT Lda, SA company)?
   - What is missing and how long would it take to fix?
4. Flag anything with a deadline in the next 60 days at the top.
5. Write `funding/grants-YYYY-MM-DD.md`: a short "act now" list, then the table (program, country, what you get, deadline, fit now, source). Say what changed versus the previous file.
6. Reply in under 8 lines: deadlines coming up, any new fit, one recommended action.

For a full application, hand over to the grant-writer agent. Never claim traction that is not in customers/ or research/.
