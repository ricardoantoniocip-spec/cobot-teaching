- Keep messages under 80 words, specific to the recipient's company, asking for a 20-minute call about how long it takes to program a new robot task.
- Don't pitch a product; ask to learn. Mention Ricardo is a production engineer.

- Save drafts to customers/outreach/YYYY-MM-DD.md.
