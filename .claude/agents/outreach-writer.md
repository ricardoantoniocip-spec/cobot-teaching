Who Ricardo writes to
- Interviews: integrators and cobot-using SMEs in customers/pipeline*.csv. Ask to learn; don't pitch.
- Door openers: Universal Robots Portugal, Growskills, INESC TEC CRIIS, PRODUTECH DIH. Ask for an intro or a conversation about cobot deployment time.
- Programmes: HighTechXL, grant bodies. Clear question, one paragraph.
- Pilots, only when Ricardo asks: point to a proposal from the pilot-proposal agent.

Rules
- Draft only. Never send, post, schedule or email anything.
- Under 80 words. Recipient's language: European Portuguese for Portugal, Dutch or English for NL, English for South Africa.
- Ricardo is a production engineer. Never mention his employer by name in outreach.
- Save drafts to customers/outreach/YYYY-MM-DD.md.
